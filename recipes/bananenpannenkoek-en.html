Banana Pancakes Recipe

Homepage

Description

Banana pancakes are the ultimate healthy breakfast treat. With just a handful of basic ingredients, you can make a stack of wonderfully sweet, fluffy pancakes. Since ripe bananas are naturally very sweet, there is no added sugar needed. This makes it not only an incredibly tasty but also a nutritious start to your day.

Difficulty: ⭐️☆☆☆☆ (Super simple)

Prep & Cook Time

  • Prep time: 5 minutes
  • Cook time: 10 minutes

Ingredients (serves 1 / approx. 4 small pancakes)

  • 2 ripe bananas (the browner the skin, the sweeter the pancakes)
  • 2 eggs
  • 50 grams of oats (or flour, for extra firmness)
  • 1 teaspoon of cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon of liquid butter or coconut oil (for frying)
  • Optional toppings: Blueberries, honey, or extra banana slices

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mash and mix: Mash the bananas completely fine with a fork on a deep plate or in a mixing bowl until you get a smooth puree. Whisk the two eggs into the mixture with a fork or a whisk.
  2. Finish the batter: Add the oats (or flour) and the cinnamon to the mixture. Stir well until you have a thick and smooth batter.
  3. Heat the pan: Heat a knob of butter or oil in a good non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Frying: Use a large spoon to scoop three or four small dollops of batter into the pan (small pancakes are much easier to flip than one big one). Fry them for about 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom is golden brown and small air bubbles form on top.
  5. Flip: Carefully flip the pancakes with a spatula and fry the other side for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden brown.
  6. Serve: Stack the banana pancakes on a plate and finish it off with your favorite toppings, like a drizzle of honey or fresh berries.

Serving tip: Warm up the blueberries briefly in a small pan with a tiny splash of water before serving. They will burst open and form a delicious, warm fruit sauce to pour over your stack of pancakes!

Enjoy your meal!
