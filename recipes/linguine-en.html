Linguine Recipe

Homepage

Description

Linguine al Limone is a fresh and elegant Italian classic that proves simplicity is the ultimate sophistication. Instead of a heavy cream sauce, the pasta cooking water combines with melted butter, fresh lemon, and grated Parmesan cheese to create a silky, glossy sauce. It is the perfect, light meal that gives you the ultimate holiday feeling within fifteen minutes.

Difficulty: ⭐️⭐️☆☆☆ (Easy / Medium)

Prep & Cook Time

  • Prep time: 5 minutes
  • Cook time: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 200 grams of linguine (or spaghetti)
  • 1 organic lemon (both zest and juice)
  • 50 grams of Parmesan cheese (finely grated)
  • 30 grams of butter
  • 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh parsley, salt, and pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta: Cook the linguine in a large pot of generously salted water until al dente according to the package instructions. Important: Right before draining, scoop out a mug of pasta water (approx. 150 ml) from the pot and save it!
  2. Create the base: Melt the butter together with the olive oil in a large frying pan over low heat. Add the lemon zest and let it gently sauté for 1 minute to release the flavors.
  3. Bind the sauce: Pour half of the reserved pasta water and the lemon juice into the frying pan. Bring this to a brief boil.
  4. Mix: Add the drained linguine to the frying pan. Sprinkle the grated Parmesan cheese on top and toss everything vigorously together over low heat. A creamy, glossy sauce will now form. Add extra cooking water if the pasta feels too dry.
  5. Finish: Season to taste with freshly ground black pepper, a pinch of salt, and sprinkle the finely chopped fresh parsley over the top.

Serving tip: This fresh pasta tastes wonderful on its own, but is even better with an extra 'bite'. Complete the dish by adding some grilled shrimp or crispy fried pancetta, and sprinkle a handful of toasted pine nuts over it right before serving!

Enjoy your meal!
