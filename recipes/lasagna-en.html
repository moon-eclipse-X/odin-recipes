Lasagna Recipe

Homepage

Description

Everyone loves it: lasagna. The ultimate comfort food and the perfect meal to come home to. Welcome at any occasion.

Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐☆☆

Prep & Cook Time

  • Prep time: 30 minutes
  • Sauce cook time: 30 to 45 minutes (longer is better!)
  • Bake time: 35 minutes

Ingredients

For the Base

  • Lasagna sheets (dried or fresh)
  • Grated cheese (Mozzarella and Parmesan cheese for the top)

For the Bolognese Sauce (Ragù)

  • Minced meat: 500 grams (beef/pork mix or pure beef)
  • Vegetables: 1 onion, 2 cloves of garlic, 1 carrot, and 1 celery stalk (finely chopped)
  • Tomato: 2 cans of peeled tomatoes or diced tomatoes (400g each) and 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • Herbs & Seasoning: 1 tablespoon of Italian seasoning (or oregano and basil), salt, and pepper
  • Extra flavor (optional): A generous splash of dry red or white wine and a splash of whole milk

For the Béchamel Sauce

  • Butter: 50 grams
  • All-purpose flour: 50 grams
  • Whole milk: 500 ml
  • Seasoning: A pinch of nutmeg, salt, and pepper

Kitchen Tools

  • Baking dish (large enough for at least 3 layers of sheets)
  • Saucepans: 1 large deep pot for the bolognese sauce and 1 smaller saucepan for the béchamel
  • Whisk: To prevent lumps in the béchamel
  • Wooden spoon / spatula
  • Knife and cutting board

Step-by-Step Instructions

The Bolognese Sauce (Ragù)

  1. Sauté the vegetables: Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a large pot. Cook the finely chopped onion, garlic, carrot, and celery over low heat for about 5 minutes until soft.
  2. Brown the meat: Add the minced meat and cook until browned and crumbled.
  3. Deglaze (optional): Pour in the red or white wine and let it reduce for 2 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes: Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute. Add the cans of peeled tomatoes and the Italian seasoning. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
  5. Simmer: Turn the heat down and let the sauce simmer gently with the lid slightly ajar for at least 30 minutes (preferably 1 hour). At the very end, optionally stir in a splash of milk for a smoother flavor.

The Béchamel Sauce

  1. Make the roux: Melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat. Add the flour all at once and stir well with a whisk. Let it cook gently for 2 minutes to cook the flour (it will start to smell slightly like cookies).
  2. Add milk: Gradually pour in the cold milk while whisking vigorously. This prevents lumps from forming.
  3. Thicken: Bring the sauce to a boil while stirring continuously. Let it simmer gently for a few minutes until you have a thick, creamy sauce.
  4. Season: Turn off the heat and stir in a generous pinch of nutmeg, salt, and pepper.

Assembling the Lasagna

  1. Preheat your oven to 200°C (390°F).
  2. First layer: Spread a thin layer of bolognese sauce onto the bottom of the baking dish.
  3. Stacking: Place a layer of lasagna sheets on top. Scoop another layer of bolognese sauce over the sheets, followed by a thin layer of béchamel.
  4. Repeat: Place another layer of lasagna sheets on top and repeat the steps until the sauces are almost finished.
  5. The top: Finish the final layer with lasagna sheets, cover them completely with the remaining béchamel (this ensures the sheets cook properly), and top it off with the grated mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.

Baking in the Oven

  1. Slide the lasagna into the preheated oven.
  2. Bake the lasagna for 30 to 35 minutes until golden brown. The cheese should bubble beautifully and the sheets should be soft (test this by poking it with a knife).
  3. Important tip: Let the lasagna rest outside the oven for 10 minutes before cutting into it.

Serving tip: Serve the lasagna with a fresh green salad.

Enjoy your meal!
